## The 5 hardest judge questions, with short honest answers

Practice these out loud. Each answer is meant to take 10 to 15 seconds. David takes deep technical questions.

**1. "Why does this need hardware? Isn't it just an app?"**
The phone is only for setup. Joining a group needs everyone at the same station at the same time, so the room does the matching, not a feed. App-only matching has been tried at this event before and didn't stick. [CHECK: confirm the earlier attempt before naming it]

**2. "How do you know people actually met?"**
A join only counts when the whole group taps in together at one station within a few seconds. Honestly, that proves they were together, not that they had a good conversation. That's why we show a reason for every match: so people know what to talk about.

**3. "What about privacy?"**
It's opt-in, first name only, no student IDs, and the badge number never shows on any screen. We delete profiles on request; we left out a self-serve delete button because without accounts anyone could have deleted anyone. And the badges we use can be copied; a real install would use secure tags.

**4. "Would anyone use this after tonight?"**
Tonight [N] people signed up and [G] groups formed, but they were hackers at a hackathon, which is a friendly crowd. The real test is a month in the Spark! lab. That's what we'd do with the equipment budget, and we'd measure how many solo people end up joining a table.

**5. "Why would people care about points?"**
Right now points are just a game: a leaderboard and a reason to meet one more person. They don't unlock anything yet. We'd like to ask you: what perk could points unlock in your lab?
